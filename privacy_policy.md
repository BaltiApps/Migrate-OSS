**Privacy Policy**

Effective Date: April 30, 2025

Migrate - Data Backup respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when the backup and restore mobile application ("App") is used.

**1. Information Collected**
- **App Data**: Data such as contacts, SMS, and call logs are accessed only as required to perform local backup and restore. This data is not transmitted and is kept in the device’s local storage.
- **No Personal Identifiable Information Stored**: Personal data is not stored or transmitted to any servers. All data processing occurs locally on the device.

**2. Permissions Required**
The App requests the following permissions to function correctly:
- **READ_CONTACTS**: To back up and restore saved contacts.
- **READ_CALL_LOG / WRITE_CALL_LOG**: To back up and restore call history.
- **READ_SMS**: To back up and restore text messages.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: To notify about backup or restore progress and results.

During SMS restore, Android requires the app to be temporarily set as the default SMS app. This is necessary to write restored messages into the system. Once the restore process is complete, the App will prompt to switch back to the previous SMS app.

These permissions are strictly used for their stated purposes. The App does not use them for unauthorized access or data collection.

**3. How Information is Used**
- To provide backup and restore functionality.

**4. Data Storage and Security**
- All backups remain on the device and are stored locally.
- No data is collected or stored on external servers.
